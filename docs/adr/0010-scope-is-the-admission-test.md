# 0010 — Scope is the admission test; freelancers are the segment

Status: accepted · 2026-08-28 · amends ADR-0002

## Decision

Redline reads a document if all three hold: the signer supplies work or
content, the other party drafted it, and a human on the other side can accept
a change. Freelancers are the segment we tune defaults and copy for. Creators
are inside the scope and outside the copy.

A document type is admitted only if the tier-one list in ADR-0004 already
covers it. Admitting one that needs a new clause type is an edit to ADR-0004,
not a scoping call.

## Alternatives

- **Creators as the segment** — ADR-0002's original call. Reversed: the folder
  holds two creator lines, both lawyers' charged prices, and no first-person
  harm account. It also fails the criterion that actually decides this, which
  is reach.
- **Audience-shaped scope.** Boundaries drawn round a person erode by
  argument; boundaries drawn round a document are testable against the tier
  list.
- **Defer the segment entirely.** Safe and honest, but "which channel opens
  first" is answerable now: freelancer communities are addressable without an
  existing audience and creator communities are not.

## Why

Part of ADR-0002's reasoning was wrong. It rejected freelancers because
nonpayment is an enforcement failure no reading tool prevents; `agent-2.md:125`
records that the harm is the *absence* of kill-fee and milestone language and
names a drafted counter-offer as the right intervention. That is a
pre-signature fix, which is exactly what this product does.

Freelancers also hold the only revealed purchasing behaviour in the research —
Fiverr contract review at $20–57, bought today — and, decisively, they can be
reached. A brand deal and a consulting agreement pass the same three
conditions and need the same taxonomy, so the segment governs defaults and
copy only.

## Consequences

- Creators are served by the scope and not claimed by the pitch. Reaching them
  later is a second landing page, not a second product.
- The $20–57 band is now our price anchor. It is evidence of demand and a
  ceiling in the same number.
- ADR-0004 gains absent payment protection in tier one, because naming this
  segment and omitting its best-documented harm would be an empty promise.
- Revisit when a creator channel opens, or when the corpus shows brand deals
  and consulting agreements need different tier orders.
